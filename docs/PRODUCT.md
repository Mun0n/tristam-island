# Product

Tristam Island is a native Android gamebook. The player moves through locations whose prose is already written. They choose an action that place offers. `IslandEngine` applies that action to the island. Nothing is generated during play.

## What this app plays

- One local save.
- The lower coast, the plateau, the white mansion, and the underground laboratory through the rescue.
- Actions, inventory, a journal, and a progress map.
- No character sheet, no clock, no health, and no die.

## Not in this repository

Dungeon Master AI, Valdeumbría, The Guardian's Bridge, and the crystal d20. That project is [dungeon-master-ai](https://github.com/Mun0n/dungeon-master-ai). The crystal die is on `feature/cinematic-d20`.
