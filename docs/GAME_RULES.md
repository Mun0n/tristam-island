# Game rules

These numbers are the rules. Changing them requires a change here and a unit test.

## Tristam Island

- The island document is schema 2. It has no character, no clock, no die, and no health.
- Hands hold at most 3 items. Item ids are unique.
- The same state and the same action produce the same result.
- A rejected action leaves the state as it was.
- The playable story is the lower coast, the plateau, the white mansion, and the underground laboratory through the communications room. The staircase door stays shut. A score is granted once. The coast points are the ones that chapter grants. On the plateau: the fountain is 1, the torch is 5, the first way through the fence is 5, the safe key is 5, the wall map is 2, each broken shop window is 2, the repaired ladder is 5, the charred island seen through the binoculars is 5, the crude door is 2, and the generators started on melted cooking oil are 5. In the mansion: entering with the code is 5, the first placard is 1, the first bedroom door opened with the screwdriver is 5, connecting the pump is 2, turning the statue is 2, opening the laboratory door is 5, riding the elevator down is 5, and the atom-bomb poster is 5. The Grant letter does not score. Listening to the locker dial is 5, crawling into the major's office is 5, taking the master key is 5, and replacing the melted radio cable is 5. The radio dial starts at 12.4020 Mc. Left subtracts 13 and right adds 20, and the band stops at 12.3895 and 12.4126. Landing on 12.4035 scores 5 once. The coast-guard call, with the headphones on, the new cable in, and the dial on that mark, scores 5 and ends the rescue. Without Tristam seen, the flight home names the neighbouring island. The hole and the tree share the fence points: the second way does not score.
